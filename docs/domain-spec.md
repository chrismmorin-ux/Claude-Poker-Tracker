#### Rule: Displayed confidence must be empirically calibrated, not merely well-formed `[model]`

- **Statement:** This system's model of decision confidence is correct iff its stated confidence level matches real-world accuracy at that level (predictions labeled "80% confident" are correct roughly 80% of the time in aggregate) — verifiable via a calibration curve built from logged prediction-vs-outcome pairs, not merely by confirming the math produces a well-formed credible interval.
- **What calibration covers** *(expanded per founder direction 2026-07-22)* — three prediction surfaces, each calibrated independently (a system can be calibrated on one and broken on another):
  1. **Villain-behavior predictions** — fold%/call%/raise% from the villain decision model, fold curves, and pool-blended stat priors. Verified against what the villain actually did on resolved decision points.
  2. **Equity and outcome claims** — hero equity / win-probability surfaced at decision time, verified against realized showdown outcomes (binary at river, distributional earlier).
  3. **Exploit confidence tiers** — the consequence-weighted tiers (exploitValidator) behind each recommendation, verified as "when the engine said high-confidence exploit, did the villain's subsequent observed behavior confirm the read?"
- **Verification method:** every surfaced prediction is logged at hand-end with its stated probability/confidence plus the resolved outcome (including showdown reveals; tagged `hero_in_hand`), per the PMC Prediction Ledger design (append-only, broad capture). Calibration curves are computed per surface over rolling windows, with predictions grouped into stated-confidence bins; a bin passes when the observed frequency falls inside the bin's own credible interval, and low-N bins report "insufficient data" rather than passing silently. Miscalibration severity scales with the consequence tier of the affected predictions (a drifted high-consequence exploit tier outranks a drifted display stat).
- **Source evidence:**
  - `system/intention.md: Imagined Outcome` — "most accurate predictive poker decisions" requires calibrated confidence
  - counterfactual — acting on "high confidence" reads that are wrong far more often than stated erodes trust that the number means anything; the founder commits stacks on these reads
  - PMC program charter (`.claude/projects/predictive-model-calibration.md`) — Prediction Ledger + hierarchical Bayesian aggregator are the designed enforcement vehicle for exactly this rule
- **Confidence:** Medium *(founder-confirmed direction 2026-07-22: "expand upon this" — rule retained and deepened; Medium until the ledger ships and produces its first curves)*
- **Review notes:** Enforcement lands with PMC Phase 5 (Prediction Ledger, IDB append-only). Until then, `modelAudit.js` rolling validation is the interim partial proxy — it validates model components, not end-to-end stated-confidence calibration. When the ledger ships, this rule's enforcement_ref should be updated to cite the ledger's calibration report.
- **Enforcement:** proposable_invariant — calibration-curve check over resolved ledger predictions (PMC Prediction Ledger, WS-172 gate → Phase 5); interim: modelAudit.js rolling validation
- **Last verified:** 2026-07-22 (founder review)
