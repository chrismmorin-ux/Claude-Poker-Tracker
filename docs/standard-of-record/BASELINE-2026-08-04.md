# Standard of Record — Baseline Audit

**Protocol:** `baseline` · **Program:** `strategy-of-record` · **Run:** 2026-08-04
**Intent event:** `ev-1785863505139-27d399bb20fd`
**Method:** static inventory of every measurement instrument in the program's declared scope

*stack declaration* — plus a consumer census of the standard's own machinery.

This is the program's first run. It establishes the reference point; it does not fix anything.

---

## 1. Headline

The standard exists and is sound. **Almost nothing is wired to it.**

The machinery in `src/utils/standardOfRecord/` is 4,480 lines across 13 modules with 304
passing tests. Six of those modules — 1,679 lines — have **zero non-test consumers**. Of 27
runnable measurement entry points in `scripts/backtest/`, **one** (`run-hero-ev.mjs`) is fully
wired to a Deal Book, a replication stamp, and the leakage guard. **Zero Result Cards exist
anywhere in the repo.** Eight research reports in `docs/` publish comparative figures and none
of them names a Result Card.

The honest reading: the standard shipped as a *specification with a test suite*, and the test
suite is what makes it look adopted. That is the exact shape of the program's own declared
`Instrumentation rot` problem class — capture that ships and is never read — occurring inside
the instrument built to detect it.

The mitigating fact, which matters: ADR-009 landed **2026-08-02**. Every research report below
predates it by 1–8 days. This is not a standard being ignored; it is a standard that has not
yet had a measurement run through it. The finding is about the *gap remaining open*, not about
anyone routing around it.

## 4. Surface registration

`buildSurfaceEntry` has exactly **one** production call site: `PBR_SURFACE` in
`poolBestResponse.mjs:482`.

`PBR_SURFACE` has **no readers**. Consumers reach for `PBR_SURFACE_ID`, `PBR_SHRINK_SWEEP`,
`PBR_WARNING`, `PBR_SCOPE` — the loose constants — and never the registry entry. So the single
registered surface in the repo is itself an unread captured field. The registry's `stack`
requirement (a throw, not a warning, deliberately per WS-324) is being satisfied exactly once
and consulted zero times.

Everything else that behaves like a surface — the engine's live recommendation path, the pool
baseline, the mined behaviour policy, the teachable-arms probe — is unregistered.

## 10. What this baseline does not cover

- **No instrument was executed.** This is a static audit. A `sweep` or `challenge` run that
  actually re-derives a published figure from its manifest would test replicability rather
  than infer it from imports. F3's falsifier is the cheapest first step.
- **`src/utils/holdingKnowledge/`** (in scope, 4 files) was inventoried for consumers but not
  scored for replicability — the WS-292 branch is mid-flight and its shape will change.
- **The Ignition extension** is out of scope by declaration but does surface numbers to the
  founder in the HUD. Whether that is a scope gap is a `blind_spot`-protocol question.
