What this is testing. Nodes sit at equal spacing regardless of their dollar value, so the track spends its pixels where bets actually cluster instead of covering a range you rarely use. Switch track to linear at a $2000 pot to feel the difference the warping makes.

Velocity replaces the wiggle. A wiggle is a low-velocity movement, so gain is driven by drag speed — no mode to enter, nothing to trigger by accident. Turn it off to feel what a fixed-gain slider gives you at a big pot.

Committed values are logged with how they were entered. Nothing here writes to the app — it is a feel test for a Gate 4 decision.
